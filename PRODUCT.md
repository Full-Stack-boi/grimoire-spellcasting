# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Game masters and players using Owlbear Rodeo during live tabletop encounters.

## Product Purpose

Grimoire provides an integrated tactical combat HUD for tracking turns and supporting spellcasting during encounters.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The BG3-style top turn tracker displays encounter order and turn state. The GM can advance or rewind turns and inspect monster HP and AC. Players must not see HP or AC in the tracker.

## Product Principles

- Keep turn order clear during live play.
- Respect the difference between GM and player information.
- Support tactical play without interrupting the tabletop workflow.
